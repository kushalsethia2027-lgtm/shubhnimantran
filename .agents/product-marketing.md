# Product marketing context — Nimantran

Version 2 · 29 October 2026. The founder explicitly replaced the wedding product with adult social events. Old wedding marketing drafts are historical and must not guide current claims.

## Product and audience

Nimantran helps adults discover and host interest-led social gatherings. Delhi NCR, with Delhi NCR search available. Primary audience hypothesis: adults looking for welcoming activities and new connections, including people arriving solo or new to the city. Hosts may be individual organisers or venue/activity operators. No customer interviews or willingness-to-pay evidence has been supplied.

Positioning to test: **Go out. Find your people.** Supporting line: **Thoda filmy. Thoda spontaneous. Very you.** Two main actions: find a gathering and host a gathering.

## Experience

Six launch categories: Naach Meri Jaan (music/dance), Khel Khel Mein (games), Daawat Hai! (food/conversations), Rang Jamao (art), Chalo Niklein (outdoors), Hello Ji! (social). Desi, contemporary, warm and playful; readable functional labels for booking and safety. Modern invitation tickets adapt to the category.

Attendees filter by interests, city/neighbourhood search, date, budget, age eligibility, atmosphere, solo-friendliness and sober preference. Map pins are approximate. Hosts state the plan, adult eligibility, venue details, food/drink inclusions, extra costs, rules and cancellation terms. Guests introduce themselves and hosts decide whether to approve. Public confirmed counts, completed-hosting counts and reviews derive from records; no fake ratings, scarcity or verification badges.

## Current evidence and limits

The code includes a new responsive frontend, Google/Supabase integration, additive RLS migration, host publication, introductions, approval, capacity checks, private exact addresses, confirmed themed SVG tickets, check-in, attendance-backed reviews, saves and reports. Local PostgreSQL and browser HTTP-fixture tests pass. Applying the migration to the hosted project, actual Google sign-in, real cross-device acceptance and Netlify publication status have not been verified.

When no live records exist, the production catalogue is empty; the homepage uses illustrated category collections. No fictional event listings are presented. Hero photography is generated brand artwork, not customer/event proof. Identity and age are not independently verified. Accounts are 18+; non-sober listings use a conservative platform age minimum of 25 and host responsibility for applicable local/venue rules. Do not claim the platform guarantees safety.

Paid approvals, checkout, payouts and refunds are not implemented. Free events can complete the booking flow after backend setup. Prior Dodo wedding-software onboarding does not establish eligibility for marketplace event payments or host payouts.

The proposed ₹250 reward for referrer and friend is coming soon, not an active offer or issued balance. Eligibility, funding, expiry, fraud controls and redemption limits must be established before launch. Do not promise automatic email/WhatsApp delivery, a social feed, chat, voice services or AI matching.

## Commercial model and acquisition

Host-set event prices; booking commission is a proposed model, not an agreed rate or validated business. No Host Pro, boosts or subscription is sold. Traffic, bookings, customers, revenue, testimonials and retention are unknown.

The founder previously confirmed a ₹0–₹5,000 monthly organic-first marketing budget; no revised budget has been supplied. Begin with real local hosts and attended gatherings, not nationwide reach claims. No posts, ads, outreach or spending are authorised merely by having a marketing plan.
